Mounts and modules

Absorbable mounts

- ›

A module that bind-mounts its own content leaves a mount any scanner can read. Absorbing re-serves it as a Prism injection and unmounts the original: same content, no mount.

Automatic; the chip is the result. Buttons handle late mounts.

When it runs

After each boot script, again at 45s; pre-zygote with my_hookless.

Hook-framework binds are never taken over.
